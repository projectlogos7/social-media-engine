# Voice

ISMII writes as an institution: measured, plain and exact. The voice follows the Institute's editorial practice in its launch film and web copy.

## Principles

**Speak as "we."** The Institute is the speaker: *We founded the Institute to keep that record.* No personal "I" in institutional copy.

**Frame affirmatively.** Say what is being built, not what is missing.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| There is no home for this field. | The field needs a home that holds it together, and we are building it. |
| Countering misconceptions about Indian Islam | Advancing an accurate account of Islam in India |
| Not just a research centre | A research centre, an archive and a training ground |

**Own interpretation.** Claims that are the Institute's judgement carry ownership language: *we believe*, *in our view*, *in our reading*. Facts carry their source.

**Calibrate confidence.** Projections say *projected*. Traditions say *tradition holds* or *is said to*. Open questions say *historians are still asking*. A claim never sounds more certain than its evidence.

**Keep it plain.** Short sentences, concrete nouns, no ornament. Prefer *the record*, *the archive*, *the scholars* to abstractions.

## Mechanics

- Name in full on first mention, then ISMII.
- No em dashes; use commas, colons, semicolons or full stops.
- Numbers: spell out one to nine in prose, figures from 10; always figures for dates, percentages and statistics.
- Dates: *19 December 2026* in formal copy.
- Transliteration: follow the source for scholarly writing; use common English spellings (Mughal, Deccan, Aligarh) in public copy.
