# Logo

## The mark

An eight-pointed star, formed by a square and a diamond, with a lobed rosette opened at its centre. All marks are true knockouts: the rosette is transparent and shows whatever ground sits behind it. *Brand pack.*

The mark comes in three registers:

| Register | Files | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full mark | `ismii-mark-sandstone`, `-ink`, `-ivory` | Every size above 40px |
| Compact mark | `ismii-mark-compact-*` | 40px and below: favicons, feed avatars, small UI. A single round aperture replaces the rosette so the mark holds together at small sizes |
| Seal | `ismii-seal` | Ceremonial use only: certificates, colophons, printed programmes. Never in interface |

## Lockups

The **horizontal lockup** is the official signature: mark, the acronym ISMII in Cormorant Garamond, a hairline divider, and the full name in Libre Franklin capitals. Use it wherever the Institute signs its work.

The **stacked lockup** is for ceremonial and centred compositions: title pages, lecture backdrops, end cards, certificates.

## Colourways

| File | Ground |
| --- | --- |
| `full-colour` | Marble Ivory, Warm Marble and other light grounds |
| `reversed` | India Ink and dark grounds. Mark and acronym in ivory, name in Muted Sand |
| `mono-ink` | Single-colour print, fax, embossing |
| `mono-ivory` | Sandstone Red, Deccani Teal, and photography |

The full-colour lockup never sits on Sandstone Red: the mark would disappear into its own colour. Use `mono-ivory` there.

## Clear space and size

Keep clear space of at least **half the mark's height** on every side. No text, edge or image enters it.

The horizontal lockup is never smaller than **160px or 40mm wide**. Below that, use the mark alone, and at 40px or less, the compact mark.

## The name

- On first mention in running text, write the name in full: *Institute for the Study of Muslims and Islam in India (ISMII)*. After that, ISMII.
- ISMII is always in full capitals, with no full stops.
- The Institute's former working names, CSII and CSIMI, are retired. Update any material that still carries them.
- Where the relationship to the College must be stated, use the affiliation line exactly: *An institute of American Islamic College · Chicago*, set in Libre Franklin capitals with open tracking.

## Keep the mark whole

Use the supplied files as they are. Keep the mark in the palette, flat, upright and unoutlined, and set the acronym only in Cormorant Garamond 600. Specifically, do not recolour the mark outside the palette, outline it, add shadows, glows or other effects, rotate it, rebuild the acronym in another typeface, or place the full-colour lockup on Sandstone Red. *Brand pack.*
