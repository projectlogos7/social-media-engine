# ISMII: brand guidelines

The Institute for the Study of Muslims and Islam in India (ISMII) is an institute of American Islamic College, Chicago. It brings the history of Islam in India and the measurable present of Indian Muslims into one scholarly home: research and fellowships, archives, public scholarship, and the training of the scholars, translators and archivists who will carry the field forward.

The identity is built to look like that work: patient, exact, and rooted in the material culture of Indo-Islamic building. Its palette is named for that culture (sandstone, marble, ink, gold, Deccani teal), and its mark is an eight-pointed star with a lobed rosette opened at its centre.

## Principles

1. **Scholarship first.** The page should read like a well-set book before it reads like a campaign. Generous margins, one idea per surface, type doing the work.
2. **One colour carries the mark.** Sandstone Red is the Institute's colour. It appears on every signature and is never diluted into gradients or tints.
3. **Marble as the ground.** Marble Ivory is the default surface. India Ink is the only dark ground.
4. **Gold is structure, never voice.** Rosette Gold and Deep Gold draw rules, frames and seals. They never set text or draw the mark.
5. **Square and measured.** Corners are square. The only circle in the system is the ceremonial seal.

## Quick reference

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Official signature | Horizontal lockup, full colour, on Marble Ivory |
| Dark grounds | Reversed lockup on India Ink; mono-ivory on Sandstone, Teal or photography |
| Clear space | At least half the mark's height on every side |
| Minimum size | Horizontal lockup 160px or 40mm wide; compact mark at 40px and below |
| Acronym | ISMII, in full capitals, set only in Cormorant Garamond 600 |
| Body and UI | Libre Franklin |
| Pull-quotes | Spectral |
| Folios and catalogue numbers | IBM Plex Mono |
| Affiliation line | An institute of American Islamic College · Chicago |

## How this system is organised

The sections below cover the logo, colour, typography, pattern and layout, voice, and applications. Tokens carry exact values: `sandstone-red`, `marble-ivory`, `india-ink` and the rest of the palette, plus two themes (`ivory` and `ink`) whose semantic tokens (`ground`, `text`, `text-body`, `brand`, `rule`, `hairline`) switch between them. The Assets tab holds every approved logo file; use those files as supplied and never redraw the mark.

**Status of this guideline.** Values marked *Brand pack* come from the ISMII brand pack and are fixed. Values marked *Proposed* (the spacing scale, the type-size scale, the companion Urdu and Devanagari faces, and parts of the applications section) are working recommendations awaiting confirmation.
