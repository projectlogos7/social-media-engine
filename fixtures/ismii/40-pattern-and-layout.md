# Pattern and layout

## The lattice

The brand's ground carries a geometric lattice of eight-pointed stars and interlaced squares, drawn tone-on-tone: a shade darker than the ground on ivory, a shade lighter on sandstone. It is visible only on a second look. *Brand pack*, as used in the banners and post frames.

- Use the lattice as a ground texture, never as a foreground graphic.
- Keep it tone-on-tone. It never takes gold, teal or a contrasting colour.
- Leave it off reading surfaces: articles, reports and long captions sit on flat marble.

## The gold rule

A single hairline of gold marks the base of a composition, separating content from signature. On post frames, all content sits **above the gold rule**; the signature sits below it. Use Deep Gold (`rule` in the ivory theme) on light grounds and Rosette Gold on dark and sandstone grounds.

## Grid and spacing

*Proposed.* Spacing runs on an 8px base (`space-1` 4px to `space-8` 96px). Use `space-6` (48px) or more as the outer margin on social formats and `space-7` or `space-8` on print and presentation surfaces. Corners are square (`radius-none`); the seal is the only circle (`radius-seal`).

## Photography

*Proposed.* Favour natural light, real places and real documents: manuscripts, building surfaces, reading rooms, people at work. Avoid heavy filters and staged stock imagery. When the mono-ivory lockup sits on a photograph, place it over a calm, dark area of the image.
